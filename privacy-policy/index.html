Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

This policy explains what information SolutionsMob ("we") collects in our Android apps, including Empty Folder Cleaner and Web Scroll Capture, why, and the choices you have.

Summary

  • We don't ask for your name, email or account. Our apps have no sign-in.
  • Your files, folders, photos and the web pages you capture stay on your device. We never upload them.
  • We use Google services for anonymous usage statistics, crash reports and ads. You can turn usage statistics off in Settings.

What we collect

Usage statistics (Google Analytics for Firebase)

To understand which features are used and where the app can be better, the app sends anonymous events such as: screens opened, buttons tapped, settings changed, whether a scan or capture succeeded or failed (with counts rounded into ranges, never exact file names or paths), ads shown, and purchases started or completed. Firebase also records basic technical information: device model, Android version, app version, language, country (derived from your IP address, which is not stored), session length and a random app-instance ID.

We never send file or folder names, file contents, web addresses you capture, or any text you type.

Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics)

If the app crashes, it sends a crash report: the technical stack trace, device model, Android version, app version and the random installation ID. You can turn crash reporting off in Settings.

Install source (Google Play Install Referrer)

When the app is first opened, it reads which store page or campaign link led to the install, so we can tell which promotions work.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free versions show ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, approximate location (from your IP address), and ad interactions, and may share them with its advertising partners to show and measure ads. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland we ask for your consent first through Google's consent message, and you can change your choice at any time from "Privacy options" in Settings. Buying "Remove ads" stops ads.

Purchases (Google Play Billing)

Payments are handled entirely by Google Play. We only receive whether a purchase succeeded, so we can unlock the feature. We never see your payment details.

Why we use it (legal basis)

  • Usage statistics and crash reports: our legitimate interest in keeping the app working and improving it. In the EEA/UK, analytics storage only runs if you agree in the consent message.
  • Ads: your consent in the EEA/UK/Switzerland; elsewhere, our legitimate interest in funding the free app.
  • Purchases: to provide what you paid for.

Your choices

  • Settings → Share usage statistics: turn off to stop analytics. Turning it off also deletes the analytics ID on your device, so earlier statistics can no longer be linked to you.
  • Settings → Crash reports: turn off to stop crash reports.
  • Settings → Privacy options (EEA/UK/Switzerland): change your ad consent.
  • Android settings → Google → Ads: reset or delete your advertising ID.
  • Uninstalling the app stops all collection.

How long we keep it

Analytics data is kept for 14 months, then deleted automatically. Crash reports are kept for 90 days. We don't combine analytics with Google Signals or any other account data.

Who we share it with

Only Google, as our service provider (Firebase, AdMob, Google Play), under Google's terms and privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy. We don't sell your data.

Children

Our apps are not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or CCPA), you can ask to access or delete data about you, or object to its use. Because we don't know who you are, the quickest way is to use the switches above. You can also contact us.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
